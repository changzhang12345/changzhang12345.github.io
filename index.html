I'm Chang Zhang, a master's student at Yale University graduating in May 2027 and a member of Yale's Inquisitive Robotics Lab, advised by Prof. Tesca Fitzgerald. My work focuses on robot learning and agentic robotics for manipulation, with interests in diffusion policies, RL post-training, and VLA. This portfolio presents selected projects across robot arms, humanoids, and dexterous hands—from simulation to real-world deployment.
